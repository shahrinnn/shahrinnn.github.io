HIERARCHIRETINA
UNDERGRADUATE THESIS01

HierarchiRetina

A collaborative research project exploring interpretable diabetic retinopathy assessment through a three-stage approach combining screening, retinal lesion and vessel segmentation, and severity grading.

My contribution: literature review, research gap analysis, methodology planning, dataset collection, EDA, preprocessing, benchmark analysis, reporting and team coordination.

ResearchData AnalysisMedical AITeamwork
Read project case study
The challenge

Diabetic retinopathy assessment is difficult to interpret when predictions do not explain which retinal changes influenced a grade.

My work

Reviewed literature, identified research gaps, helped plan the methodology, collected datasets, performed exploratory data analysis and preprocessing, created results visualizations, evaluated benchmarks, maintained references, and coordinated team progress.

Research approach & outcome

The three-person thesis developed and evaluated a hierarchical screening → lesion/vessel segmentation → severity grading framework across multiple public datasets. This is team research; I do not claim to have personally trained the models.
